An unadorned link with a trailing arrow, used beside a primary button or to close a section.

- **Markup:** `<a class="text-link">Label <span aria-hidden="true">↓</span></a>`. Supply the label and arrow (↗ ↓ →), with a 20px gap.
- **Hover:** turns `red` in dark and `red-text` in light. There is no underline at rest; underlines are kept for links inside prose and the email link in the closing band.
- Sits in an `.actions` row, `actions-gap` (26px) after the button.
